Which IELTS test is right for you?

Start by thinking about why you need an English test. Your destination, organisation, or visa route may determine which IELTS test you need.

♧

IELTS Academic

For people applying to higher education or professional registration where academic-level English is needed.

▣

IELTS General Training

For people moving to an English-speaking country for work, training, or other eligible migration routes.

⌂

IELTS for UKVI

A test option for certain UK visa and immigration applications. Check the exact requirements for your route.
